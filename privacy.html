Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you use the Video_Tiktok application and connect your TikTok account, we access certain information authorized by you via the official TikTok Login Kit and Content Posting API:

  • Basic Account Information: Your TikTok Open ID, display name, and avatar URL to identify your connected publishing profile.
  • OAuth Credentials: Secure access tokens and refresh tokens necessary to authenticate publication requests.
  • Content Data: Video files, captions, and publishing preferences created by you to be transmitted to TikTok.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information strictly for the following purposes:

  • To authenticate and maintain your connected TikTok account session.
  • To initialize and complete video uploads to your TikTok profile or inbox drafts as requested by you.
  • To check the status of your video publishing jobs and display confirmation to you.

We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal data or TikTok account information to any third parties or advertisers.

3. Data Storage & Security

Your OAuth tokens are encrypted at rest using industry-standard authenticated encryption algorithms (AES-GCM / Fernet) in our local secure vault. Tokens are transmitted strictly over secure HTTPS connections directly to TikTok's official API endpoints (open.tiktokapis.com).

4. Data Retention and Deletion (User Data Deletion Instructions)

You have full control over your data. If you wish to revoke access or request deletion of your stored credentials and data:

  • Disconnect via App: You can disconnect your TikTok account at any time in the Accounts section of the application by clicking "Remove Account". This immediately purges all encrypted tokens from local storage.
  • Revoke via TikTok: You can revoke our application's access anytime through your TikTok account settings under Settings and privacy > Security > Manage app permissions.
  • Data Deletion Request: To request complete deletion of any associated metadata, send an email to support@videotiktok.app with your TikTok Open ID or username. We will process your deletion request within 48 hours.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or inquiries regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact our Data Protection Officer at: support@videotiktok.app.
